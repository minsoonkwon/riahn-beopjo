Perms & Straightening

Prices for root perms and iron perms, volume straightening, AVEDA natural perms, Shiseido perms and men's perms.

Home/Perms & Straightening

Root & volume straightening
to AVEDA natural and Shiseido perms

Our signature menus are root perms and iron perms focused on root volume. Prices are grouped from bangs perms and straightening, root straightening and root perms (iron) to premium perms and Shiseido & AVEDA perms, setting and volume straightening heat perms, repair perms and Shiseido perm + Shiseido perm treatment sets.

With AVEDA natural perms — and AVEDA color, a 98% natural color available during pregnancy — guests who are pregnant or have bleached or very damaged hair can also be served after a consultation. There is also a separate men's menu, including iron perms, volume straightening and down perms. For aftercare, see Treatment & Scalp Care.

Recommended for

  • Guests who want root perms or iron perms for root volume
  • Guests who want sleeker hair with volume or root straightening
  • Guests who are pregnant or have bleached or very damaged hair and want to consult about AVEDA natural perms
  • Men looking for iron perms, volume straightening or down perms

FAQ

Perm FAQ

Can I get a perm during pregnancy?
AVEDA natural perms are available during pregnancy after consultation. Shiseido & AVEDA natural root straightening is ₩140,000, the Shiseido & AVEDA perm starts at ₩100,000 (S) and the Shiseido & AVEDA setting perm starts at ₩120,000 (S).
How much is volume straightening?
Basic volume straightening is S ₩100,000 – LL ₩130,000, premium is S ₩120,000 – LL ₩150,000, and Shiseido & AVEDA volume straightening is S ₩140,000 – LL ₩170,000. Root straightening is ₩100,000 (basic), ₩120,000 (premium) and ₩140,000 (Shiseido & AVEDA natural).
Do you have a men's perm menu?
Yes. Men's premium perm ₩70,000, men's specialty perm (foil / spin swallow) ₩100,000, men's down perm (sides) ₩20,000, boys' perm ₩60,000, men's volume straightening & iron perm ₩100,000 (basic) and ₩120,000 (premium).
