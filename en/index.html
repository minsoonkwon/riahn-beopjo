About

From consultation to finish,
one-on-one with a head director

RIAHN Hair Geomdan Beopjo Town is on the 3rd floor of the building right next to Exit 6 of Ara Station (Incheon Line 2), 6 Ieum 4-ro, Geomdan-gu, Incheon — close to the Wondang-dong and Dangha-dong neighborhoods of Geomdan New Town. Four head directors, each with 10+ years at brand salons, handle every service themselves, one-on-one from consultation to finish, with no interns.

As a Shiseido concept salon, we offer cuts, perms, color and treatments. With AVEDA natural perms — and AVEDA color, a 98% natural color available during pregnancy — guests who are pregnant or have bleached or very damaged hair can also be served after a consultation.

Our signature menus are root perms and iron perms focused on root volume, plus custom wigs, top pieces, scalp covering and the EN FORET head spa. We also have a separate men's menu for cuts, perms and color. Opened in 2025 with 9 styling chairs, we offer free parking and are open every day of the year, 10:00–20:00.
