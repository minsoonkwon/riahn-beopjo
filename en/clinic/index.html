Home/Treatment & Scalp Care

Shiseido & AVEDA treatments
and the EN FORET head spa

As a Shiseido concept salon, we offer basic, pre- & post-service and premium treatments, Shiseido & AVEDA treatment, repair treatment and Shiseido treatment packages (3 sessions, or 5 + 1 free).

For the scalp we offer scalp care, deep scalp care and head spa courses (20, 40 and 60 min), and you can consult about the EN FORET head spa, custom wigs, top pieces, hair extensions for volume and scalp covering all in one place. Cut sets are on Haircuts.

Recommended for

  • Guests who want to care for hair damaged by perms or color with a repair treatment
  • Guests who want regular Shiseido treatments with a package
  • Guests who want to care for the scalp with scalp care or a head spa
  • Guests who want to consult about custom wigs, top pieces or scalp covering
