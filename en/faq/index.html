FAQ

Answers to common questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book a date and time on Naver Booking. You can also contact us by phone (+82-32-565-4642) or Instagram DM (@riahn_seobu_beopjo).
Is parking available?
Yes. Free parking is available.
What are your hours and closing days?
We are open every day of the year, 10:00–20:00.
Where is the salon?
#312–314 (3F), KR Beopjo Tower, 6 Ieum 4-ro, Geomdan-gu, Incheon — the building right next to Exit 6 of Ara Station (Incheon Line 2).
Who will do my hair?
Four head directors with 10+ years at brand salons handle every service themselves, one-on-one from consultation to finish, with no interns.
How much do services cost?
Cuts ₩23,000–₩28,000 (cut sets ₩45,000–₩60,000), perms & straightening ₩20,000–₩220,000 (repair perm from ₩300,000), color ₩40,000–₩250,000, and treatment & scalp care ₩30,000–₩500,000 (including Shiseido packages). See each service page for menu prices.
Can I get a perm or color during pregnancy?
AVEDA natural perms and AVEDA color, a 98% natural color, are available during pregnancy after consultation. Guests with bleached or very damaged hair can also be served after a consultation.
Do you have student prices?
We have student cuts: ₩25,000 for women and ₩23,000 for men.
Can I book a specific head director?
Please choose your preferred head director when booking. You can see each head director's specialties on the Our Designers page.
Can I consult about wigs or scalp covering?
Yes. You can consult about custom wigs, top pieces, hair extensions for volume, scalp covering and the EN FORET head spa all in one place. These are Choi Mina's specialties.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during business hours (every day 10:00–20:00).
