Women's, men's and student cuts
to cut + head spa sets

At RIAHN Hair Geomdan Beopjo Town, four head directors with 10+ years at brand salons cut your hair one-on-one, from consultation to finish, with no interns. Mika specializes in Zone & Section design cuts, men's design cuts, hush cuts and sleek cuts; Hyemi in tassel cuts and layered bobs; and Suhyeon in Vidal Sassoon design cuts for women and men, short cuts, bobs and layered cuts.

Women's and men's cuts come in regular and design options, and we also offer student and kids' cuts and cut + scalp care or head spa sets. For more volume, see Perms & Straightening.

Recommended for

  • Guests who want an easy-care layered bob or tassel cut
  • Guests looking for a face-flattering short cut or bob
  • Men who want a design cut, hush cut or sleek cut
  • Guests who want scalp care or a head spa with their cut
