AVEDA natural color
to bleach, balayage and ombré

We offer AVEDA color — a 98% natural color available during pregnancy — for root color, full color and men's color. We also have regular and premium color, bleach, hair manicure / color wax / henna, sombré and ombré, and Shiseido color treatment sets.

Hyemi specializes in bleach and balayage, bright gray coverage and even root color without patches; Mika, an AVEDA colorist, in milk brown and brown without red tones. For aftercare, see Treatment & Scalp Care.

Recommended for

  • Guests who are pregnant and want to consult about AVEDA natural color
  • Guests who want brown or milk brown without red tones
  • Guests who want bright color with bleach, balayage or ombré
  • Guests who want even root touch-ups without patches

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

Can I get AVEDA natural color during pregnancy?
Yes. AVEDA color is a 98% natural color and is available during pregnancy after consultation. AVEDA natural root color is ₩60,000 (1 cm) or ₩70,000 (3 cm), and AVEDA natural full color is S ₩80,000 – LL ₩130,000.
How much is men's color?
Men's root color (1–2 cm) is ₩40,000, premium ₩50,000 and AVEDA natural ₩60,000. Men's full color is ₩50,000 (regular), ₩60,000 (premium) and ₩70,000 (AVEDA natural), and men's bleach is ₩80,000.
How much is bleach?
Bleach is S ₩80,000 – LL ₩110,000, and sombré / ombré is S ₩100,000 – LL ₩130,000. Bleach (ombré / sombré) + color + Shiseido color treatment is ₩250,000.
