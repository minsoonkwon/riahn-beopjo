Natural, undetectable
wigs, hair volume and scalp covering

Beyond perms, color and cuts, RIAHN Hair Geomdan Beopjo Town offers consultations for custom wigs, top pieces, hair extensions for volume, scalp covering and the EN FORET head spa. Choi Mina specializes in natural-looking custom wigs and hair extensions for volume, scalp covering for a wide forehead or M-shaped hairline, scalp care and help with hair-loss concerns.

In the salon you can consult about custom wigs without an inner cap, natural fashion wigs, volume pieces, wigs for chemotherapy periods and partial wigs for women and men, as well as Hi-Mo and Milan products. You can pick up custom wigs from RIAHN WIG, RIAHN Hair's wig brand, at this salon and have them cut, permed and colored here.

Recommended for

  • Guests looking for a partial wig or top piece for thinning at the crown or front
  • Guests who want a natural, undetectable custom or fashion wig
  • Guests who want to consult about a wig to wear during chemotherapy
  • Guests who want to cover a wide forehead or M-shaped hairline with scalp covering

Good to know about RIAHN WIG custom wigs

  • Wigs are made within 10 days of ordering on average; this may vary with order volume and options.
  • Each wig is handmade, with hair set strand by strand.
  • The standard size is 16 cm × 21 cm; if your head is much larger or smaller, we consult on a custom-made wig in the salon.
  • The standard color is natural black, and you choose the gray-hair ratio when ordering. Other colors can be done in the salon.
  • When ordering from the RIAHN WIG online store, you can choose pickup at RIAHN Hair Geomdan Beopjo Town.
  • Online wigs are shipped with the hair left long, so we recommend a detailed cut fitted to your head in the salon.
  • Once production of a made-to-order wig has started, refunds for a change of mind are limited.

FAQ

Wig FAQ

What kinds of wigs can I consult about?
You can consult about custom wigs without an inner cap, natural fashion wigs, volume pieces, wigs for chemotherapy periods and partial wigs for women and men, as well as Hi-Mo and Milan products. Hair extensions for volume and scalp covering are Choi Mina's specialties.
Can I pick up a RIAHN WIG custom wig at this salon?
Yes. When ordering from the RIAHN WIG online store, you can choose pickup at RIAHN Hair Geomdan Beopjo Town. Wigs are made within 10 days of ordering on average.
Can wigs be cut, permed or colored?
Yes. In the salon you can have your wig cut, permed and colored, set and maintained, and reinforced. Please ask the salon for prices.
How much are wigs?
Based on RIAHN WIG online store prices: men's custom wigs ₩399,000–₩499,000, women's full wigs ₩499,000–₩599,000, women's crown cover wigs ₩149,000–₩189,000, and women's hairpieces ₩69,000–₩99,000.
